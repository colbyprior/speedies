# Neutral Units

As part of Events, Neutral Units will appear on the board. They act independently of the players, although some may help some players while hinder others. There are a number of general rules for Neutral Units, which are described below.

## General Rules

The following section describes some general concepts that apply to all Neutral Units, impacting how they interact with others and are interacted with themselves.
### Unit Disposition

Neutral Units can either be Hostile or Neutral towards player's warbands. If they are Hostile, they will generally actively seek out and attack that warband. If they are Neutral, they will ignore that warband.

If Neutral Units are ever targeted by a Spell, or attacked in any way, they will become Hostile to the offending unit's warband (if they weren't already). If you are Aligned with them, you become Unaligned with them. Note that this rule does not apply to Neutral Heroes hired to fight against you by an opponent. In this case, you remain Aligned with them for the duration of the game.

Unless explicitly stated, an Unaligned Neutral Unit is Hostile to each warband. If a Neutral Unit is Aligned with a warband, it is assumed to be Neutral, unless otherwise stated.

#### Aligning with Neutral Heroes

You may attempt to Align with a Neutral Hero if all of the following are true:
* Both you and your opponent are Unaligned with the Neutral Hero
* You are not Aligned with the Neutral Hero that opposes it (if it has an opposing Neutral Hero)
* You were not Aligned with the Neutral Hero at any point during this game

To attempt to become Aligned, one of your units may make a Morale Check during your Cast Phase if they are within 5" of the Neutral Hero. Only one such attempt can be made by a warband per turn.

On a failure, nothing happens. On a success, you become Aligned with the Neutral Hero. If it was Hostile to you, it becomes Neutral to you. Note on your warband sheet that you are Aligned with the Neutral Hero.

If you become Aligned with a Neutral Hero, any Neutral Units that it is allied with (such as the Necromancer's zombies), also become Neutral towards you.

### Triggering Units

Often in Events, 'Triggering Units' are mentioned. This is simply the unit that picked up the Treasure that triggered the Event. 'Triggering Warband' is the warband of the 'Triggering Unit'.

### Injuries

Neutral Units generally handle Injuries the same way as other units. Neutral Heroes will usually have the 'Heroic' skill, meaning that Ranged Attacks that would Incapacitate them only Stun them instead.

### Neutral Units Being Engaged

If a Neutral Unit is being Engaged and another Neutral Unit could Intercept the Engage, they will do so.

## Neutral Unit Turn

A typical Neutral Unit turn is described in this section. If there are conflicting rules specific to a Neutral Unit, they take precedence over the rules stated below.
### Upkeep Phase

1. Recover: Neutral Units recover just like any other unit does.
2. Treasure: Unless stated otherwise, Neutral Units ignore Treasure. If it is explicitly stated that a Neutral Unit picks up Treasure, this is when they do so. They make a Morale Check as normal if the Treasure is contested by any unit that they are hostile towards, as per the normal rules.
3. Ongoing Spells: Should a Neutral Unit have an Ongoing Spell, they make a Morale Check as normal.
4. Determine Target: Neutral Units will often 'Target' a unit. Unless explicitly stated, the Target is the closest Hostile unit by distance (rather than the closest one via Movement) that can be reached without any Agility Checks. This can include units that are not in Line-of-Sight. If there are no such units, it is the closest Hostile unit that can be reached with the fewest Agility Checks.

### Engage Phase

1. If the Neutral Unit is within Engage distance of the Target, they will Engage it, unless Retreating. The Neutral Unit does not need to perform Out-of-Sight Rolls even if the Target is not within Line-of-Sight

### Move Phase

1. If the Neutral Unit cannot Engage, it instead makes a Move toward the Target, moving as directly as possible toward it.
	1. The Neutral Unit does not move in a way that would require an Agility Check, unless stated otherwise
	2. The exception is if the Target is unreachable unless the Neutral Unit performs an Agility Check. In this case, the Neutral Unit moves towards the Target in a way that requires performing the least number of Agility Checks to reach the Target

#### Retreating

If a Neutral Unit is Retreating, it treats the closest point that's a board edge as the Target for the purposes of Moving. It still follows the conventions for Neutral Unit movement (generally not making Agility Checks). If the board edge is reached, it is immediately removed from the board.

### Cast Phase

1. If the Neutral Unit has any Spells or Abilities, it performs them now, as described in the Neutral Unit's rules. If a hostile target is required, the Target is used.
	1. If the Target cannot be targeted, it will instead target the closest Hostile Unit within Line-of-Sight, if it is within range.
### Ranged Phase

1. If the Neutral Unit is not Engaged and has a Ranged Weapon, it will make a Ranged Attack against the Target, if it has Line-of-Sight and is within range.
	1. If the Target cannot be attacked, it will instead target the closest Hostile Unit within Line-of-Sight, if it is within range.

### Melee Phase

2. If the Neutral Unit is Engaged, resolve that Melee combat.
	1. This may mean that units may take part in Melee combat multiple times before it reaches that unit's turn again, similar to multiplayer games
	2. If they defeat a unit that has a Treasure in Melee combat, they will take that Treasure. As usual, they are Slowed and cannot carry more than one Treasure.
