## Vampire vs Hunter

| **Opposed ↓  /  Triggering →** | **Aligned Vampire** | **Aligned Hunter** |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Aligned Vampire**            | Hunter Chaos        | Duel               |
| **Aligned Hunter**             | Duel                | Hunt Vampire       |

### Duel

The vampire and hunter both appear next to a hero of their aligned warband of their choice. Each of the units are now fully under the control of their aligned warband and will count towards their route thresholds.
### Hunter Chaos

Use the same logic as the Trigger Hunter scenario but he is hostile to both warbands.
### Hunt Vampire

The vampire is skulking in the shadows of the battlefield. Each time a unit enters into a building they must roll morale.
- If they fail the roll the vampire appears as though they engaged the unit that entered the building.
- At the end of the melee phase the vampire will skulk into the shadows again.
## Blight Prophet vs Inquisitor

| **Opposed ↓  /  Triggering →** | **Aligned Blight Prophet** | **Aligned Inquisitor** |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Aligned Blight Prophet**     | Cleanse with Fire          | Duel                   |
| **Aligned Inquisitor**         | Duel                       | Stop the Blight        |

### Duel

The vampire and hunter both appear next to a hero of their aligned warband of their choice. Each of the units are now fully under the control of their aligned warband and will count towards their route thresholds.
### Cleanse with Fire

Place the Inquisitor inside the building nearest to the centre of the table. All buildings except for this building are now on fire.

### Stop the Blight
The Blight Prophet appears at the center of the board. All chests which have not been picked up yet including the chest that triggered the event immediately move to the Blight Prophet. Each turn the Blight Prophet will cast Gift of Blight but the AOE will be 10”.

## Necromancer vs High Mage

| **Opposed ↓  /  Triggering →** | **Aligned Necromancer** | **Aligned High Mage**    |
| ------------------------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Aligned Necromancer**        | The High Mage Cleanses  | Duel                     |
| **Aligned High Mage**          | Duel                    | A Necromancer’s Paradise |

### Duel

The vampire and hunter both appear next to a hero of their aligned warband of their choice. Each of the units are now fully under the control of their aligned warband and will count towards their route thresholds.
### The High Mage Cleanses
Use the same logic as the Trigger High Mage scenario but he is placed at the top of the highest building and is hostile to both warbands.
### A Necromancer’s Paradise
The Necromancer is placed as per the normal rules. He is hostile to both warbands.
Every time any non-Zombie unit is Incapacitated, immediately summon a Zombie in its place. If it is placed into melee, it will not make any attacks that turn, but can be targeted.

## Shady Merchant
Trigger "Shop" if both warbands are aligned with the Shady Merchant.
### Shop
The Shady Merchant sets up shop in the centre of the board. He is selling an Artifact, the Wand of Teleportation. It can either be bought for the price of one Treasure, or can be stolen which will cause that warband to no longer be aligned with the Shady Merchant.

If a unit is adjacent to the Artifact and is holding a Treasure during the Treasure step of the Upkeep Phase, they may give it to the Shady Merchant in exchange for the Artifact.

The Artifact counts as a treasure for the purposes of contesting it. If a unit is buying it legitimately, it may only be contested by another unit that is also attempting to buy it legitimately. It can be bought at the end of the game using the Grabbing Treasure after Routing rules, but cannot be stolen this way.

If the Artifact is bought, the Shady Merchant will retreat with the Treasure. If it is stolen, he will become hostile to that warband and attempt to Steal back the Artifact before retreating.

If the wand has already been purchased and is held by a warband when this is triggered the Shady Merchant teleports the wand back into his hand so that he may sell it again.

## Pit Fighter
Trigger "Single Combat" if both warbands are aligned with the Pit Fighter.

### Single Combat
Both players simultaneously elect one of their Heroes as their Champion. Immediately move both units to the highest walkway or bridge on the map, placing them adjacent to each other. This counts as a Pit Fight between the two Champions, with the warband that triggered the event counting as having Engaged the other unit. Should either Champion Incapacitate the other, the losing warband immediately routs.

Other units not part of the Pit Fight act as normal. It is possible to Rout by the usual means before the single combat concludes. If this happens, the game ends, but the Pit Fight continues until it is resolved. The loser of the Pit Fight becomes the new Pit Fighter.
