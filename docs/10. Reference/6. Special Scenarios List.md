## The Blight

*The forces of the Blight Prophet are trying to spread the Blight throughout the district. It's up to the Inquisition to contain it.*

**Inquisitor Objective:** Protect the bystanders while they retreat

**Blight Prophet Objective:** Spread the Blight by Incapacitating the bystanders in Melee

**Map:** Dense City

**Deployment:** Deploy as normal with the Inquisitor side choosing the edge to deploy on and deploying first. Eight Bystanders are then set up along the same board edge. They are all 6" in from the board edge and are evenly spaced along it (so they are 4" away from each other and the two on each end are 4" away from the side board edges).

**Turn Order:** Inquisitor warband, Blight Prophet warband, Neutral

**Rout Threshold:** Warband Rout Threshold + 3

**Game End Treasure:** The Inquisitor warband gains one Treasure for every Bystander that Retreats or would have Retreated if they had taken another turn. The Blight Prophet warband gains one Treasure for every Bystander Incapacitated.

**Special Rules:**

*Bystanders*: There are eight Bystanders set up at the start of the game. Each turn, they Retreat towards the Blight Prophet player's starting board edge. They have 6" Move and no Defence. They do not fight back in Melee and automatically Disengage at the start of their turn. They are Neutral to the Inquisitor player and Hostile to the Blight Prophet player.

If they would be Incapacitated outside of Melee, they are Stunned instead. For every Bystander that is Incapacitated, this lowers the Inquisitor player's Rout Threshold by 1. Incapacitated Bystanders do not give Improvements. For every Bystander that successfully Retreats from the board, this lowers the Blight Prophet player's Rout Threshold by 1.

*Neutral Heroes:* At the end of the Upkeep Phase, a Neutral Hero may target a Bystander within 15" and make a Morale Check. On a success, the Neutral Hero's player may move the Bystander 3" in any direction. This may not cause an Agility Check.



## Coup

*Neutral Heroes of both sides try to depose each other to gain power.*

**Faction Objectives:** Incapacitate the enemy Neutral Hero while allowing their own Neutral Hero to Retreat

**Map:** Two parallel streets in dense city with the centre of each street 9" from the closest board edge (see diagram).

**Deployment:** Roll to determine deployment order as normal. The first player sets up within 2" of either of the board edges where the streets start/finish. The player places their Neutral Hero along the board edge so that it's in the middle of one of the two streets. The second player sets up within 2" of the opposite board edge, then places their Neutral Hero along the board edge so that it's in the middle of the other of the two streets.

**Turn Order:** First player's Neutral Hero, first player, second player's Neutral Hero, second player

**Rout Threshold:** Warband Rout Threshold + 3

**Game End Treasure:** If a player's Neutral Hero successfully Retreats, they gain 3 Treasure. If a player's Neutral Hero is Incapacitated, their opponent gains 3 Treasure.

**Special Rules:**

*Neutral Heroes:* Players do not have direct control over their Neutral Hero during the battle. Instead, before each player's turn (including the first one), each Neutral Hero moves along the street towards the opposite board edge from where they started, Retreating towards that point. Neutral Heroes will always Disengage from Melee at the start of their turn. If they have Ranged Weapons, they can use it.

If a Neutral Hero successfully Retreats, it lowers the opponent's Rout Threshold by 3. If a Neutral Hero is Incapacitated, it lowers their ally's Rout Threshold by 3 (rather than the usual 1).


## Jailbreak

*The Shady Merchant has just broken out of jail. The alarm has been raised, but can the guards catch them in time?*

Shady Merchant Objective: Have the Shady Merchant Retreat

Opponent Objective: Capture the Shady Merchant

Map: Dense City with a jail in the middle.

Deployment: Four Hooded Figures are placed directly in the centre of the board. The Shady Merchant warband deploys around them and within 5" of the centre of the board. The opposing warband deploys within 2" of all board edges. Neither side deploys their Neutral Heroes at the beginning of the game.

**Turn Order:** Neutral, Shady Merchant's warband, Opposing warband

**Rout Threshold:** Warband Rout Threshold + 3

**Game End Treasure:** If the Shady Merchant successfully Retreats, they gain 3 Treasure. If the Shady Merchant is Incapacitated, their opponent gains 3 Treasure.

Special Rules:

*Hooded Figures:* The Shady Merchant's warband has four Hooded Figures, which are Neutral units. Chosen randomly, one of these is the Shady Merchant. This information is secret to both players. After each Shady Merchant players turn, each one Retreats towards a different corner of the board. They have Move 6 and have no Defence. Hooded Figures are Neutral to all factions. If Hooded Figures would be Incapacitated outside of Melee, they are Stunned instead. 

*Revealing Hooded Figures:* When a unit of the opposing warband is within 5" and Line-of-Sight of a Hooded Figure, it is immediately revealed whether it is the Neutral Hero or not. If it's not, it drops a Treasure Token where it was and is removed from the board.

Whenever it becomes certain what identity the remaining Hooded Figures are (either because it must be the Neutral Hero or because the Neutral Hero is revealed), the remaining Hooded Figures are revealed. Should a Hooded Figure successfully Retreat off of any corner, it is revealed but no Treasure Token is placed.

*Revealing the Neutral Hero:* When the Shady Merchant is revealed, it becomes controllable by the Shady Merchant's player (unless it has already Retreated). When this happens, the opposing player immediately places their Neutral Hero anywhere on a board edge, then places two Faction Henchman either side of it (these Henchman are in addition to any that may have been hired by the player).

The Shady Merchant can Retreat off of any corner once it is revealed. If it successfully Retreats at any point, it reduces the opposing player's Rout Threshold by 3. Should it be Incapacitated, it reduces the Shady Merchant player's Rout Threshold by 3.

The Neutral Heroes may or may not be able to act on their next turn (or the current turn) depending on when the Shady Merchant was revealed. Use the table below to determine when they can next act.

| Reveal Turn            | Shady Merchant          | Opposing Neutral Hero   |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Neutral                | No actions on next turn | Can act as normal       |
| Shady Merchant Warband | No actions this turn    | No actions on next turn |
| Opposing Warband       | Can act as normal       | No actions on this turn |



## Secure the Artifact

*All factions have gotten wind of the location of a powerful artifact - a sword of immense power. Who can secure it for themselves?*

**Faction Objectives:** Secure the artifact, then use it to make the other factions rout.

**Map:** 3.5' by 4' of dense city. After setting up the map, place the artifact so that it is in the middle of one of the 4' edges chosen at random (ie. 2' from the 3' edges) and 14" away from the 4' edge.

**Deployment:** There are three valid deployment zones. Two are in the closest corners to the artifact and the final one is in the middle of the 4' edge further away from the artifact. Roll for deployment order. Each player then selects and deploys in each zone, in order. Players can deploy within 5" of the corners or middle of the edge (as appropriate).

**Turn Order:** Turns are taking in deployment order, as usual.

**Rout Threshold:** Warband Rout Threshold + 3

**Game End Treasure:** The winner of the match gains 1 Treasure (as usual for winning) and the Artifact, second place gains 2 Treasures.

**Special Rules:**

*The Artifact:* The Artifact is a two-handed melee weapon of great power. It has the following stats:

| Name         | Mel | Inj | Prc | Special Rules | Slots |
| ------------ | --- | --- | --- | ------------- | ----- |
| The Artifact | 7   | 7   | 3   | +1 Attack     | 2     |

It can be picked up using the same rules as Treasure, but does not trigger Blight or Event checks. Units with the 'Vanguard' Skill can pick it up, but cannot be the first unit of the battle to do so. Units that cannot use equipment cannot pick the Artifact up.

Once picked up, any unit that can use equipment can immediately use the Artifact in place of their own melee weapons. After the game, it is a treated as a Rare Weapon (so can only be used by Heroes). While the Artifact is being held by a warband's unit, it reduces the Rout Threshold by 3 of all other warbands.


## The Arena

*A showmatch is being held in the pit fight arena*

**Faction Objectives:** Win the battle

**Map:** A 2' by 2' board contains an 18" by 10" arena. 2" walls surround it, upon which four quadrants of grandstands sit. The arena itself is mostly clear, with only a few pillars or other minor terrain inside it.

**Deployment:**

**Deploying Pit Fighters**: Both players choose three of their heroes to partake in the arena battle. If they do not have at least three heroes, they take all of the heroes and also take any henchman of their choose until they picked three units. Determine at random which end each side will start at. Deploy the three heroes, plus the Neutral Hero in a line along the middle of the 10" wall. The other player then does the same on the opposite wall.

**Deploying the Crowd**: Roll to determine deployment order as normal. Each player then takes turns to place a single remaining unit of their warband at a time. To do so, they roll a d20 to randomly determine one of the four quadrants, then places the unit anywhere within that quadrant. This continues until both sides have placed all of their remaining units. Should one player finish placing all of their units before the other, the other player places all of their remaining units one at a time, rolling randomly each time to determine the quadrant.

**Turn Order:** First player, Second player

**Rout Threshold:** Warband Rout Threshold + 3

**Game End Treasure:** For every one of the enemy Pit Fighters that are Incapacitated, the player gains one Treasure.

**Special Rules:**

*The Crowd*: The Crowd cannot be equipped with any equipment during this match. They are instead Unarmed, but do not suffer any penalties in Melee they may usually suffer while being Unarmed. They cannot act until the Brawl starts.

*The Pit Fight:* The first phase of the Special Scenario is the Pit Fight. Only the Pit Fighters may act each turn. They may not attempt to climb the 2" walls surrounding the Arena or attempt to leave it in any way. They may not target any units in the Crowd. Pit Fighters count as two units towards Rout Checks.

*The Brawl*: As soon as one side Incapacitates two of the enemy Pit Fighters, their units in the Crowd may all make an immediate Engage attempt, following the usual rules. At this point the second phase starts and all units (both the Crowd and the Pit Fighters) act as normal.
