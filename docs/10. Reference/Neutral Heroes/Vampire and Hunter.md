## A bloody proposal

The vampire steps from the shadows next to the treasure and makes an offer.

* **Let him feed:** The unit that triggered this event is Incapacitated. The vampire is now hostile towards the opposing warband.  
  * The Incapacitated unit must roll for Scars with Disadvantage at the end of the game  
  * This counts as **1** towards his Hunger  
  * Increase the vampires progress track by **1**  
  * The vampire is now **aligned** with your warband  
  * The opposing warband may optionally **align** with the hunter  
* **Refuse:** The vampire appears 1” away from the unit that triggered the event and is now hostile to both warbands.  
  * Your warband is now **aligned** with the hunter  
  * The opposing warband may optionally **align** with the vampire

## Trigger Vampire

The vampire appears on a random board edge and is **hostile** to your warband.

* If the opposing warband is unaligned with the Vampire they may optionally align with them

## Trigger Hunter

The hunter becomes **hostile** to your warband and will appear at the highest floor of a random building on the map. The hunter will remain on this floor of the building and attempt to shoot the triggering warband.

The hunter uses the following logic in order:

* They will not willingly leave this floor of the building  
* They will engage any hostile unit on the floor of their building  
* They will move if required and target the nearest hostile hero with a ranged attack  
* Otherwise they will move if required and target the nearest hostile henchmen with a ranged attack

If they are forced out of their building, they instead act as normal.

* If the opposing warband is unaligned with the Hunter they may optionally align with them

## Duel

The vampire and hunter both appear next to a hero of their aligned warband of their choice. Each of the units are now fully under the control of their aligned warband and will count towards their route thresholds.

* If the hunter survives and the vampire is incapacitated they gain an additional 1 on their progress track. The hunter aligned warband gains an extra step towards patronage.  
* If the vampire survives and the hunter is incapacitated they gain an additional 2 on their progress track. The vampire aligned warband gains an extra step towards patronage.

## Hunter Chaos

Use the same logic as the **Trigger Hunter** scenario but he is **hostile** to both warbands.

## Hunt Vampire

The vampire is skulking in the shadows of the battlefield. Each time a unit enters into a building they must roll morale.

* If they fail the roll the vampire appears as though they engaged the unit that entered the building.  
  * At the end of the melee phase the vampire will skulk into the shadows again.  
  * If the vampire is incapacitated or the **Hunger** count reaches the threshold, he will no longer re-appear.

# Special Scenario

Feed the vampire:

* Civilians scatter the map.  
* The vampire can incapacitate a civilian in melee to add to the opponent route threshold.  
* The allied warband can pick up civilians like treasure to bring them to the vampire.  
* If the opposed warband takes out the vampire it counts towards 3 units in the route threshold.
