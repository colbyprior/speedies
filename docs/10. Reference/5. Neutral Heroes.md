# Neutral Heroes

Neutral Heroes are special characters that can be hired by any warband for a cost.

## Blight Prophet
*Hero* — _Blight Prophet_

*Cost:* 45g

A herald of the Blight, spreading its gift to all who draw near.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9| 15| - | 18| 10| 10| 1 | 2| 0| 0| Gift of Blight

This unit is equipped with a Dagger. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Gift of Blight
As a difficulty 10 spell, the Blight Prophet will cause a 5" AOE of blight. Each unit in range must make a morale check or they will contract the blight. The Blight Prophet will attempt to cast this spell every turn even if it would target allies only. If he is ever Incapacitated, he automatically casts this spell.

##### Event
* Spawn within 3" of the Event chest.

##### Logic
* He casts Gift of Blight every turn, even if it would only catch allies.
* Each turn he Moves towards the largest group of units within 12".
* If he is Incapacitated he automatically casts Gift of Blight.

##### Patronage
* Ensure three units contract the Blight from the Blight Prophet.

## Inquisitor
*Hero* — _Inquisitor_

*Cost:* 90g

A holy warrior of the church, wielding divine power to heal allies and purge the Blight.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9| 14| - | 12| 10| 10| 1 | 1| 3| 0| Healer, Cure Blight

This unit is equipped with a Sword, Shield, Heavy Armour. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Healer
This unit can use the 'Heal' Divine Magic spell.
##### Cure Blight
Once per battle this unit can attempt the Cure Blight spell. Difficulty 10, range 10". The target unit is cured of the Blight.

##### Event
* Spawn on a random edge of the nearest building to the Event chest.

##### Logic
* If he starts his turn next to a unit with the Blight, he will cast Cure Blight.
* Each turn he will move towards the nearest building and light it on fire.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Inquisitor heals the Blight off one unit.

##### Pit Fighter
When recovering, this unit goes from Stunned straight to Staggered.

##### Event
* Spawn within 3" of the nearest pair of Engaged units, or the Event chest if none are Engaged.

* Each turn he Engages the nearest unit, preferring one that is already Engaged.

##### Long Reload 
You may not use this weapon if it was fired in the previous turn.
##### Steal
If this unit ends its Move within 1" of an enemy carrying Treasure, it may make an Agility Check. On a success, it takes the Treasure from that unit.
##### Slippery
Enemy units have Disadvantage on Agility Checks made to Intercept this unit.

##### Event
* Spawn on the board edge nearest the Treasure token furthest from the Event chest.

##### Logic
* Each turn he Moves towards the nearest unit carrying Treasure and attempts to Steal it.
* If he is carrying Treasure he instead Retreats, leaving the game once he reaches a board edge.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Shady Merchant leaves the board carrying a Treasure.

## Vampire
*Hero* — _Vampire_

*Cost:* 95g

A vampire stalking the streets of Blightmeer.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7| 14| - | 18| 10| 10| 2 | 2| 3| 0| Undead, Fear, Hunger

This unit is equipped with Dual Swords. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Undead
This unit may not be slowed. When Engaging, this unit has a bonus +3" to its Move speed. If this unit would be Stunned, it is instead counted as Dazed. This unit is immune to the Blight.
##### Fear
This unit causes Fear.
##### Hunger
Count how many wounds / incapacitations this unit causes this battle. When it reaches 2, they will disappear from the board.

##### Event
* Spawn in contact with the nearest terrain piece that blocks Line-of-Sight to the Event chest.

##### Logic
* He Targets the nearest unit that is not Undead and performs Normal Actions.
* If a Target is within Engage distance he will always Engage rather than Move.
* Once Hunger reaches 2, he disappears from the board at the end of his turn.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Vampire Incapacitates two units in a single battle.
