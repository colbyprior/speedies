# Neutral Heroes

Neutral Heroes are special characters that can be hired by any warband for a cost.

## Blight Prophet
*Hero* — _Blight Prophet_

*Cost:* 45g

*Opposed by:* [Inquisitor](#inquisitor)

A herald of the Blight, spreading its gift to all who draw near.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11| 15| - | 18| 8| 10| 1 | 2| 0| 0| Heroic, Gift of Blight

This unit is equipped with a Dagger. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Gift of Blight
As a difficulty 10 spell, the Blight Prophet will cause a 5" AOE of blight. Each unit in range must make a morale check or they will contract the blight. The Blight Prophet will attempt to cast this spell every turn even if it would target allies only. If he is ever Incapacitated, he automatically casts this spell.

##### Event
* Place the Blight Prophet within 3" of the Event chest.

##### Behaviour
* The Blight Prophet casts Gift of Blight every turn, even if it would only catch allies.
* If the Blight Prophet is Incapacitated he automatically casts Gift of Blight.

##### Patronage
* Ensure three enemy units contract the Blight this battle.

## High Mage
*Hero* — _Elf_ — _High Mage_

*Cost:* 40g

*Opposed by:* [Necromancer](#necromancer)

An elven archmage who holds his ground and lets the weather do the killing.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9| 12| - | 18| 10| 10| 1 | 2| 0| 0| Heroic, High Mage

This unit is equipped with a Staff. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### High Mage
The High Mage has the spells listed on his Spells card. The spells are all difficulty 10 for the High Mage. If a player has hired him, roll 2d20 and select one of the two results to determine the spell Cast. If he appeared through an Event, use his Cast Priority instead. These spells never affect the High Mage.

##### Spells
| Roll | Spell | Effect |
| ---- | ----- | ------ |
| 1-4 | Earthquake | All units within 5" make an Agility Check or make a Mishap Roll. |
| 5-8 | Meteor | Choose a target unit within 15" and Line-of-Sight. All units within 3" of the target, including the target, must make a Mishap Roll. |
| 9-12 | Thunderstorm | All units carrying Treasure must make a Mishap Roll. |
| 13-16 | Hurricane | All units within 1" of a ledge must make an Agility Check or fall. |
| 17-20 | Flood | Hits all units on the ground. Agility Check, with Disadvantage if wearing Armour, or make a Mishap Roll. |

##### Cast Priority
When not under a player's control, the first condition met determines the spell Cast.

1. Enemies within 5" — Earthquake.
2. The Necromancer or the enemy leader within 15" and in Line-of-Sight — Meteor, targeting them.
3. Three or more Treasure held by enemies — Thunderstorm.
4. Three or more enemies within 1" of a ledge — Hurricane.
5. Otherwise — Flood.

##### Event
* Place the High Mage in the centre of a random board edge.

##### Behaviour
* During the Cast Phase the High Mage makes a Morale Check. If he passes, consult the Cast Priority on his Spells card.
* Once per turn you may assist the High Mage in the cast phase within 5" of him. This will give him advantage on his next spellcasting check.

##### Patronage
* If the High Mage successfully casts 4 spells this game to gain Patronage.

## Hunter
*Hero* — _Hunter_

*Cost:* 60g

*Opposed by:* [Vampire](#vampire)

A trapper who rigged every chest in the district before anyone arrived.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8| 12| 12 | 14| 10| 10| 2 | 2| 3| 0| Heroic, Spring Traps

This unit is equipped with a Sword, Torch. This is already included in the stat table above.

| Ranged Weapon | Rng | Inj | Prc | Special Rules |
| ------------- | --- | --- | --- | ------ |
| Crossbow | 30" | 2 | 0 | Long Reload |
##### Long Reload 
You may not use this weapon if it was fired in the previous turn.
##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Spring Traps
While this unit is on the board every Treasure is trapped, including the Event chest. A unit makes an Agility Check to pick up a Treasure, and at the start of each turn it carries one. On a failure, determine a random direction and move 3" — the Treasure on a pickup, which then fails, otherwise the carrier and its Treasure together. A unit moved off a ledge Falls. Incapacitate this unit to disarm the traps.

##### Event
* Place the Hunter in a random unoccupied building on the top floor.

##### Behaviour
* The Hunter Targets the nearest unit carrying a Treasure, otherwise the nearest Undead or Blighted unit.
* The Hunter holds position and shoots, Moving only to regain Line-of-Sight to his Target.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Hunter's traps move a Treasure or carrier five times.

## Inquisitor
*Hero* — _Inquisitor_

*Cost:* 90g

*Opposed by:* [Blight Prophet](#blight-prophet)

A holy warrior of the church, wielding divine power to heal allies and purge the Blight.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9| 14| - | 12| 10| 10| 1 | 2| 3| 0| Heroic, Cure Blight

This unit is equipped with a Sword, Shield, Heavy Armour. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Cure Blight
Once per battle this unit can attempt the Cure Blight spell. Difficulty 10, range 10". The target unit is cured of the Blight.

##### Event
* Place the Inquisitor on a random edge of the nearest building to the Event chest.

##### Behaviour
* If the Inquisitor starts his turn within 5" of a unit with the Blight, he will stay and cast Cure Blight.
* Otherwise, each turn the Inquisitor will move towards the nearest building and light it on fire.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Inquisitor heals the Blight off one unit or burns three buildings.

## Pit Fighter
*Hero* — _Pit Fighter_

*Cost:* 80g

A veteran of the pits, hardened by countless brutal fights.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9| 12| - | 16| 10| 10| 2 | 1| 3| 0| Heroic, Pit Fighter

This unit is equipped with a Sword x2. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Pit Fighter
When recovering, this unit goes from Stunned straight to Weakened.

##### Event
* Place the Pit Fighter next to the unit that triggered the event. They are now in a Pit Fight, engaged with the Pit Fighter.
* No one else can target the Pit Fighter or triggering unit for any purpose, until either unit is Incapacitated, at which point the winner is declared.
* You may only align with the Pit Fighter if you defeat him in a Pit Fight.
* If you lose: If the unit is a hero, it’s now in the Pits! It will replace the current Pit Fighter as the Pit Fighter Neutral Hero until it can escape (see the 'Pit Fight' Scar Table entry for more information). The Pit Fighter is removed from the board with the Treasure.

##### Patronage
* Defeat the Pit Fighter in combat a second time.

## Shady Merchant
*Hero* — _Shady Merchant_

*Cost:* 70g

A merchant of questionable morals who always seems to have exactly what you need... for a price.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10| 14| 14 | 18| 6| 10| 2 | 1| 3| 0| Heroic, Steal, Slippery

This unit is equipped with a Sword x2. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Steal
If this unit ends its Move within 5" of an enemy carrying Treasure, it may make an Agility Check. On a success, it takes the Treasure from that unit.
##### Slippery
The Shady Merchant will never Engage another unit and will always Move on his turn. All attacks against him are made with Disadvantage. He automatically disengages at the end of each melee phase, moving 1” directly away from units he is Engaged to.

##### Event
* Place the Shady Merchant on the board edge nearest the Treasure token furthest from the Event chest.

##### Behaviour
* Each turn the Shady Merchant Moves towards the nearest unit carrying Treasure (friend or foe) and stops to Steal it as soon as he is in range.
* If the Shady Merchant is carrying Treasure he instead Retreats, leaving the game once he reaches a board edge.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Shady Merchant leaves the board carrying a Treasure.

## Vampire
*Hero* — _Vampire_

*Cost:* 95g

*Opposed by:* [Hunter](#hunter)

A vampire stalking the streets of Blightmeer, drawing the night in behind him.

| Mov | Mel | Rgd | Def | Agi | Mrl | Atk | Wnd | Prc | Inj | Skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7| 14| - | 18| 10| 10| 2 | 2| 3| 0| Heroic, Undead, Fear, Nightfall

This unit is equipped with Dual Swords. This is already included in the stat table above.

##### Heroic
If this unit would ever be Incapacitated by a Ranged Attack, it is Stunned instead.
##### Undead
This unit may not have its Move reduced below its base Move. When Engaging, this unit has a bonus +3" to its Move speed (which can be reduced). If this unit would be Stunned, it is instead counted as Dazed. This unit is immune to the Blight.
##### Fear
This unit causes Fear.
##### Nightfall
Everything within 15" of this unit is in Darkness. Ranged Attacks into, out of, or within Darkness have Disadvantage. Engaging a target in Darkness requires an Out-of-Sight Roll, even with Line-of-Sight. This unit, and any unit carrying a Torch, are never in Darkness.

##### Event
* Place the Vampire in a random building on the highest floor.

##### Behaviour
* The Vampire Targets the nearest unit that is not Undead or an animal and performs Normal Actions.
* Where possible, the Vampire will prefer to make attempt diving charges and out of sight rolls.

##### Patronage
* Ensure the Vampire Incapacitates two units in a single battle.
