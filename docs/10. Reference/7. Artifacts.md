# Artifacts

Artifacts are powerful magical items that can be acquired during campaigns.

## Assassin Garb

**Effect:** Armour. +1 Def. Add +5 on Out-of-Sight Rolls. Cannot be used with a Shield.


## Boots of Haste

**Effect:** +2 Move


## Cloak of Elvenkind

**Effect:** Advantage on all Agi checks.


## Ethereal Robe

**Effect:** Gain the 'Ethereal' skill

##### Ethereal
This unit may move through terrain and other units as if they weren't there, but may not ends its movement inside of terrain or other units. It must still make Out-of-Sight Rolls as normal and must move through terrain or units when Engaging if this will make the path to its target shorter. It may be intercepted by enemies as normal. It may not Protect others from Ranged Attacks.

## Giant Slayer

**Effect:** Great Sword (2 handed). You have Advantage on Melee rolls against units with more than 1 max Wounds.


## Holy Relic

**Effect:** Opponent's Rout Threshold is lowered by 1.


## Mask of Madness
**Cursed: Cannot unequip**

**Effect:** Berserker, Ambush, Fear

##### Berserker
At the end of the Engage Phase, if there is an enemy within Line-of-Sight and Engage range and this unit has not already attempted to Engage, this unit attempts to Engage the closest such unit.
##### Ambush
This unit gets Advantage on the first round of combat when they Engage an enemy.
##### Fear
This unit causes Fear.

## Wand of Fireball

**Effect:** Spell DC 10. Range 10". AOE 3" diameter mishap rolls.


## Wand of Gust

**Effect:** Spell DC 10. Range 15". AOE 3". Units get pushed away from the caster 5".


## Wand of Polymorph

**Effect:** Unit can Cast the following Spell: "Polymorph. Difficulty 15. 15". Ongoing. On a success, the target is turned into a Giant Rat, only retaining its current Wounds."
