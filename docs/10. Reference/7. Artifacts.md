# Artifacts

Artifacts are powerful magical items that can be acquired during campaigns.

## Assassin Garb
*Artifact*

Dark clothing designed for stealth and protection.

**Effect:** Armour. +1 Def. Add +5 on Out-of-Sight Rolls. Cannot be used with a Shield.

**Special Rules:** Add +5 on Out-of-Sight Rolls. Cannot be used with a Shield.


## Boots of Haste
*Artifact*

Enchanted boots that greatly increase the wearer's speed.

**Effect:** +2 Move


## Cloak of Elvenkind
*Artifact*

An enchanted cloak that grants supernatural agility.

**Effect:** Advantage on all Agi checks.


## Ethereal Robe
*Artifact*

A ghostly robe that allows the wearer to phase through obstacles.

**Effect:** Gain the 'Ethereal' skill

##### Ethereal
This unit may move through terrain and other units as if they weren't there, but may not ends its movement inside of terrain or other units. It must still make Out-of-Sight Rolls as normal and must move through terrain or units when Engaging if this will make the path to its target shorter. It may be intercepted by enemies as normal. It may not Protect others from Ranged Attacks.

## Giant Slayer
*Artifact*

A legendary blade forged to fell the mightiest foes.

**Weapon Type:** Great Sword

**Effect:** Great Sword (2 handed). You have Advantage on Melee rolls against units with more than 1 max Wounds.

**Special Rules:** Advantage on Melee rolls against units with more than 1 max Wounds.


## Holy Relic
*Artifact*

A sacred artifact that bolsters your warband's morale while breaking the enemy's will.

**Effect:** Opponent's Rout Threshold is lowered by 1.


## Mask of Madness
*Artifact* — **Cursed: Cannot unequip**

A cursed mask that grants terrible powers at a steep price.

**Effect:** Berserker, Ambush, Fear

##### Berserker
At the end of the Engage Phase, if there is an enemy within Line-of-Sight and Engage range, this unit attempts to Engage the closest such unit.
##### Ambush
This unit gets Advantage on the first round of combat when they Engage an enemy.
##### Fear
This unit causes Fear.

## Wand of Fireball
*Artifact*

A powerful wand that unleashes explosive fireballs.

**Effect:** Spell DC 10. Range 10". AOE 3" diameter mishap rolls.


## Wand of Gust
*Artifact*

A wand that conjures powerful gusts of wind to reposition foes.

**Effect:** Spell DC 10. Range 15". AOE 3". Units get pushed away from the caster 5".


## Wand of Polymorph
*Artifact*

A dangerous wand that can transform enemies into harmless creatures.

**Effect:** Unit can Cast the following Spell: "Polymorph. Difficulty 15. 15". Ongoing. On a success, the target is turned into a Giant Rat, only retaining its current Wounds."
