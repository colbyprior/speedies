# Update Neutral Heroes

## Increase Favour

If you were the winner of the game, increase your Favour with each Neutral Hero you are aligned with by one. Select one of those Neutral Heroes and add a second, additional Favour.

If you were the loser of the game, increase your Favour with two Neutral Heroes you are aligned with by one.

If you have maxed out Favour (ie. 12 Favour) with a Neutral Hero, they become your Patron! Notify the other players, so that they are aware they can no longer have that Neutral Hero as a Patron. If you already have a Patron, you cannot take a second Patron.

When a Neutral Hero becomes your Patron, you gain the following benefits:
* You may hire your Patron at half the cost as normal via Rally Support
* You always have first choice to hire them via Rally Support before others
* No one can hire your Patron to fight against you via Rally Support

## Neutral Hero Progress

After each game, the winner of that game rolls for Scars and makes a free Improvement Attempt for any Neutral Hero that took part, no matter how they joined (Event, Support, Special Scenario etc.).

Neutral Heroes can gain Deathtouched and Blight as usual. Neutral Heroes cannot learn new Skills or Spells and cannot go over stat caps as normal.

## Neutral Hero Death

If a Neutral Hero dies it is permanently removed from the campaign. All warbands that were Aligned with that Neutral Hero no longer are. If that unit had an opposing Neutral Hero, it automatically maxes out its progress track and gains the usual benefits from it.
